Show 8 views in a grid of 2 rows by 4 columns, all the same size and same height, evenly spaced, nothing overlapping, full body visible in each. Top row left to right: front, front three-quarter facing right, right side profile, back three-quarter facing right. Bottom row left to right: back (we see the tail, the belt knot and the back of the gi), back three-quarter facing left, left side profile, front three-quarter facing left. The gi must be plain flat mint green with no pattern, logos or text. Transparent background, no shadows, no labels, no text. Landscape image.
